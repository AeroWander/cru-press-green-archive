## Viewing God at Work

This article was written by Mark Gauthier to Missionalteam leaders to give perspective and explanation on the changes in the statistics made by the National team fall of 2006. We want you to hear the heart of why we record God’s work on our campuses. We also think it is essential that you know what we have chosen to measure and why we believe it is important.

## Celebrating the Works of God

Throughout the ages, the works of God have been a cause for celebration and the foundation of faith for God’s people. In the Old Testament, the Psalmists’ example spurs us to:

Commemorate the Works of God Commemorate: To keep in people’s memories

I will remember the deeds of the Lord; yes, I will remember your miracles of long ago. [Psalm 77:11]

Meditate on the Works of God Meditate: To think deeply about.

I will meditate on all your works and consider all your might deeds. [Psalm 77:12] Great are the works of the Lord; they are pondered (or studied; ESV) by all who delight in them. [Psalm 111:2]

Celebrate the Works of God Celebrate: To mark with rejoicing and praise

For you make me glad by your deeds, O Lord; I sing for joy at the works of your hands. [Psalm 92:4]

Narrate the Works of God Narrate: To give a written or spoken account of

One generation will commend your works to another;

the will tell of your might acts... They will tell of the power of your awesome works, and I will proclaim your great deeds. [Psalm 145.4, 6]

So also in the New Testament, the first disciples recorded (for example, the book of Acts) and reported God at work through their ministry.

“...and they declared all that God had done with them, and how he had opened a door of faith to the Gentiles.” (Acts 14:27; ESV)

“...they were welcomed by the church and the apostles and elders, to whom they reported everything God had done through them.” (Acts 15:4)

Along with the inherent value for stimulating our worship and faith, recording and reporting how God is at work also has leadership value.

## Leaders Lead

From honest evaluation A key role of the leader is to embrace the reality that comes from accurate measurements. Evaluating those measurements enables us to identify areas where God is obviously working and where we need to see change.

Toward increasing fruitfulness Reality is the fertile ground for change. As we live out the roles and responsibilities of a leader, knowing the facts is a key in enabling us to lead toward greater effectiveness.

In light of the whole We are each called to live and lead in light of the whole of the task we have been given. Measurements enable us to be wise and faithful stewards of our campuses, our regions, the entirety of the US campus ministry and ultimately to the global cause.

## Our Success Criteria

The following represents the Success Criteria of the US Campus Ministry.

Personal and Group Evangelism How many people have heard the gospel through personal evangelism? (Includes both staff and student evangelism and personal internet evangelism.)

What was the total number of people who heard the gospel through group evangelism? (For instance: team meetings; evangelistic presentations; etc.)

Media Exposures How many people have been exposed to significant gospel content with opportunities to respond in the last month through media exposures?

(For instance: myeverystudent.com/ES.com, FSKs; Bible, book or magazine giveaways; etc.)

Decisions for Christ How many people have indicated a decision to receive Christ as their Savior and Lord?

Multipliers How many students are currently involved in spiritual multiplication at the most organic level? That is: (a) seeking to grow in Christ; and (b) seeking to share their faith and pass on to others the foundational concepts of the Christian living they are receiving?

Student Leaders How many student leaders are leading small groups of Win, Build, Send ministry? (For instance: groups focused on reaching target audiences, launching movements, etc)

The following numbers will be updated at the end of the semester/quarter or as changes occur.

Movement Profiles Movement definition: A leader with five aligned students involved in Win, Build, and Send on the most organic level. We are measuring the number of movements planted in order to get to the scope of our mission.

### Number of Students Regularly Involved in Cru

Number of Laborers Sent A graduated Christ-centered laborer is someone who is growing in his/her faith, is multiplying his/her life, and has been sent with a personal strategy to help fulfill the Great Commission.

Additional Notes You will note several significant changes in these criteria. Two in particular may need additional information.

The omission of the Involved New Believer statistic In the 1990’s this statistic was added in order to solve the systemic problem of students’ lack of engagement in the mission. We believed that by measuring the end result of evangelistic efforts it would actually increase our effectiveness in seeing students come to Christ and get involved in our ministry. While this is a very good statistic, almost 15 years later we find we have the same problem to solve. Now what we are doing is measuring several key events in the process of seeing a student become involved in our mission, instead of assuming those events.

The definition of Laborers Sent It has always been our passion as a movement to “send” each and every student as a Christ- centered laborer. We want to more accurately measure our effectiveness in doing this. This number still includes the number of staff, stinters and interns Joining with us, which is vital to the growth of our movement and the accomplishment of the mission to which God has called us.
