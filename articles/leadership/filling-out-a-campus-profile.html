Filling Out a Campus Profile

By Tom Virtue

392 words · 2 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A worksheet by Tom Virtue for gathering demographic and spiritual-climate data on a college campus before planting a ministry, with fill-in-the-blank prompts covering enrollment, student makeup, housing, social hangouts, existing Christian groups, campus political/moral climate, and prevailing spiritual attitudes.

Launching a new ministry

Before planting a restaurant in a new location, a company like McDonalds spends months gathering critical data and surveying local demographics. Nothing of that magnitude is necessary for planting a campus ministry but due diligence is required. At the bare minimum planning and preparation requires a basic survey of the campus. This survey will inform what strategies you employ as you seek to establish a ministry on campus.
