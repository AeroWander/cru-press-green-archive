Campus Ministry Volunteer Application

385 words · 2 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A blank Campus Ministry Volunteer Application form for prospective 'key' volunteer leaders, gathering contact and background details and asking questions about spiritual maturity, personal character, moral standards, and leadership ability, plus two references.

The purpose of this form is to help determine where a person is in the process of growing in their walk with Christ. We are not looking for perfection but rather a pattern of growth.

Spiritual Maturity 1. Share briefly when and how you became a Christian.

  1. Comment on the growth you’ve seen in your life over the last year. (Please list some specific areas in your life where you have grown personally).
  2. Have you ever been discipled by an individual? If so, please comment on this.
  3. What is your understanding of initiative evangelism? What is your attitude toward initiative evangelism?
  4. How would you describe your devotional life? (time with God in prayer, reading the Bible, etc.) Personal Maturity Because we are looking for ‘Key’ Leaders, it’s important these people are modeling a godly character and lifestyle. Your answers will be strictly confidential.
  5. What do you consider to be your greatest strengths? (Please be specific.)
  6. What are areas of needed improvement and development?
  7. Comment on your ability to follow through with projects. (responsible, self-motivated, creative, a procrastinator?)
  8. Describe your moral standards in dealing with the opposite sex. Have you compromised these standards? Please explain.
  9. Comment on your use or nonuse of alcoholic beverages or other drugs. Please explain. Leadership Development
  10. How do you respond to authority, both internally and externally? (That is, how do you feel and act?)
  11. Comment on your ability to work with other people. Are you a “loner” or a “people gatherer”; “the life of the party,” etc.?
  12. What are qualities or characteristics that other people would use to describe you? How would people describe what it’s like to work with you?
