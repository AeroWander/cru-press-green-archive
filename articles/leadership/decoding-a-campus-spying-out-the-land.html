Decoding a Campus– Spying Out the Land

By Tom Virtue

352 words · 2 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A short guide by Tom Virtue listing ten practical ways to 'decode' or survey a new campus before launching a ministry there, including prayer walking, gathering maps and materials, finding an insider escort, photographing hangout spots, researching student life and worldviews, and identifying receptive 'persons of peace.'

Launching a new ministry

album. Shoot pictures of the places where people hang out or party.

  1. What’s it like to live there? Lodging, shopping, transportation.
  2. University life: Academic disciplines. Get a copy of the school calendar.What is the campus identity? What sports are big?

When you show up on campus, what should you want do? One thing that would be helpful is a little decoding, or spying out of the land. Check out Nehemiah 1:3-4, and Numbers 13:1,17-20 to get a picture of this.

  1. Student life: What do they do for fun? What are the hang-outs? Where do they live? Where are they from? What organizations, associations, and sub-cultures? The key is to get an understanding of what God is doing and where He wants to work so that we can partner with Him in all that He is doing.
  2. Worldviews and ideologies: Which ones are represented on campus? Use surveys and focus groups. Ask questions and listen.

Top Ten Ways to Decode a New Campus

  1. Prayer walking: Praying on site with insight for God to work and for God to give us a vision, a burden, and ideas. Nehemiah (1:3-4) sat down and wept when he saw and felt a burden. The Holy Spirit will give you an emotional and spiritual burden.
  2. Persons of Peace (Luke 10:1-12): Like Lydia in Acts 16, people who are receptive to us and to our message, who might be used by God to reach their “oikos” (social webs) and be the core of a future transformational community. They may or may not be a Christian yet. Receptive and influential. “You meet one person and you meet a group.” When rejected, keep going.
  3. Gather stuff: Maps, brochures, catalogues and other info about the campus.
  4. Get an escort: Find someone who knows their way around, with an insider’s view. Pray for this!
  5. Look for Great Commission resources: God’s people are here! Look for Christian students, a church, or Christian faculty. “Do you know any students who go to a Bible study or church?”
  6. Get pictures of the campus: Maybe make a photo
