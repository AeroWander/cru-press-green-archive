MAIN CATEGORY: LAUNCHING A NEW MINISTRY

Sub-Category Heading: Building a Movement

Sub-Category Text:

The ingredients that lead to a growing movement are not complicated as long as you remember that when to do things is as important as what things to do. This is why the resources in this section try to follow a chronological order. This can get tricky when activities overlap, but there is room in the order and timing for flexibility. The order of activities that are suggested here provide a helpful structure from which you may choose to deviate.

Document Descriptions:

Step 1: Funding First

Funding First

Description: It’s hard to envision building a movement with a bank

account of zero. Not that it can’t be done, but why even attempt it?

Tags: Movement Building, Funding, Raising Support

Step 2: The Planning Process

Planning Process

Description: In the Campus Ministry, leaders are required to plan for the upcoming school year. The process we equip all leaders with is called the Strategic Planning Process.

Tags: Movement Building, Planning, Strategic Plan, Campus Plan

Step 3: Prepare Your Team

Orienting Your Team

Description: What is critical here is to gather together any and all members of your missional team before the school year begins for a time to pray, plan, and divide up first week responsibilities.

Tags: Movement Building, Missional Team, Planning, Preparation, Semester

Step 4: Connect With Servant Team

Servant Team

Description: Most campuses refer to the core group of student leaders as the Servant Team. You may call them whatever you want, but it’s important to you need to meet with them at the very start of the year to pray and plan.

Tags: Movement Building, Servant Team, Planning, Preparation, Leadership, Semester

Step 5: The Leadership Retreat

Leadership Retreats

Description: We just discussed the importance of your Missional Team meeting to pray and plan, and gathering your Servant Team to do the same. With much to be discussed, it’s a good idea to hold these meetings over the span of several days—mini leadership retreats.

Tags: Movement Building, Planning, Preparation, Leadership Retreat, Semester

Step 6: Planning Part 1

Planning Part 1

Description: As you plan for the year, and bring others into that planning at the leadership retreats, it is important to map out both ‘what’ you are going to do and ‘how’ you are going to do it.

Tags: Movement Building, Planning, Preparation, Leadership Retreat, Semester

Step 6.5: Planning Part 2

Planning Part 2

Description: With the ‘what’ and the ‘how’ mapped out, factor into your planning the ‘when’ as well. Different times of the year are better for different ministry objectives.

Tags: Movement Building, Planning, Preparation, Leadership Retreat, Semester, Cycles of Momentum

Step 7: The First Few Weeks

The First Few Weeks

Description: Weeks 1–3. The first few weeks offer a unique opportunity that will not be duplicated during any other time of the school year. What is accomplished in these weeks will set the trajectory for the entire year.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Gathering, Surveys

Step 8: 30-Second Surveys

30-Second Surveys

Description: Weeks 1–3. Perhaps the most significant activity of these first few weeks is the Dinner-line Survey. It allows you to gather hundreds, even thousands of filtered contacts.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Gathering, Surveys, Dinner-line, Freshman

Step 9: Publicity

Publicity

Description: Weeks 1–3. Posters, flyers, surveys, rent a blimp if you need to but use every avenue of promotion available to make your ministry as visible as possible in the first few weeks on campus.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Gathering, Publicity, Posters, Advertising

Step 10: Gathering Bible Studies

Gathering Studies

Description: Weeks 1–3. Gathering Bible Studies provide a critical holding-tank for students wanting to get involved. They provide an event you can publicize and invite-to, while postponing the thoughtful process of placing people in the ‘right’ Bible Study.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Publicity, Quad Groups, Gathering, Small Groups

Step 11: The Men’s and Women’s Overnight

Men’s and Women’s

Description: Weeks 4–6. Having a Men’s/Women’s overnight between the start of the semester and the Fall Retreat provides a stutter-step of involvement: an opportunity to get to know people better.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Overnight, Men’s Time, Women’s Time

Step 12: Launch Small Groups

Small Group Structure

Description: Weeks 4-6. Having gathered as many new people as possible, you now want to place them in a Bible study that will be best suited to their commitment and maturity level. This explains a way to structure your Small Groups.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Small Groups, Challenge, Structure, Action Group, Training Group, Discovery Group

Step 13: Establish Discipleship Relationships

Discipleship

Description: Weeks 4–6. Discipleship, like all mentoring, is an intensive investment of time and energy. Good stewardship demands selectivity, and as a leader it is important to give consideration to who should receive such an investment.

Tags: Movement Building, Discipleship, Selection, The Compass

Step 14: The Fall Retreat

All About the Fall Retreat

Description: Congratulations, you made it. The past weeks of ministry have been one long sprint to the Fall Retreat. The Fall Retreat is not simply one more event. It is the event that consolidates all of your labor up to this point.

Tags: Movement Building, Fall Retreat, Recruiting

Step 15: Time For An Outreach

Time For Outreach

Description: Late October–Early November. Having consolidated your ministry at the Fall Retreat, the ministry’s focus must be turned outward toward the mission of reaching lost students.

Tags: Movement Building, Evangelism, Outreach

Step 16: Emphasis on Prayer

Prayer Initiative

Description: Late October–Early November. The impending evangelistic emphasis provides the opportunity to give lift to the student prayer movement. Take advantage of it and spotlight prayer.

Tags: Movement Building, Prayer, Fireseeds

Step 17: Socials and Community Building

Socials

Description: Late November–Early December. This time of year lends itself to holding some creative socials, holiday parties, and other special times of community building. But don’t forget the other focus of this time: recruiting to Christmas Conference.

Tags: Movement Building, Socials, Community, Parties, Holidays

Step 18: The Christmas Conference

The Christmas Conference

Description: The Christmas Conference accelerates the growth and commitment of students, solidifies student leadership, and students return to campus ready and eager to lead, serve, and reach the campus for Christ.

Tags: Movement Building, Christmas Conference, Challenge Recruiting

Step 19: The Second Semester Rally

Second Semester Rally

Description: Late January–Early February. Your job at the start of the semester is to rally the ministry and bring them together as a committed community, “standing firm in one spirit, contending as one man for the faith of the gospel” (Phil. 1:27).

Tags: Movement Building, Second Semester, Community, Unity

Step 20: Time For Another Outreach

Outreach

Description: Mid–Late February. Remember, times of building up should always be followed by times of reaching out. And so at some point in February it is a good idea to think in terms of a major ministry focus on evangelism.

Tags: Movement Building, Evangelism, Outreach

Step 21: Big Break

Spring Break

Description: March. The Mid-point of second semester is a final mile-marker before the end of the year. Hang on, you’re almost there.

Tags: Movement Building, Big Break, Training, Spring Break, Sending

Step 22: Sending Focus

Sending Focus

Description: Last 6 Weeks. The last six weeks of the year is a good time to focus on

the “sending” component of the ministry, such as preparing Seniors for graduation, recruiting to Summer Projects, and challenging students to leadership for next year.

Tags: Movement Building, Sending. 100% Sent, Summer Projects, Seniors

Step 23: Evaluating The Ministry

Evaluating The Ministry

Description: To wrap up this year and set the trajectory for next year, give attention to evaluation: getting honest input from student leaders and your missional team.

Tags: Movement Building, Evaluation, Planning, End of Year

Step 24: Ending Well

Ending Well

Description: There’s only one way to properly wrap-up the school year.

Tags: Sleep, Sleepy, Sleeping, Snoring
