Developing a Proposal

By Laura Viau

403 words · 2 min read · Article · For staff & team leaders

In brief

A guide by Laura Viau on writing a fundraising proposal and executive summary for ministry partners, built on a strategic plan and budget. Provides a worksheet covering organizational background, the current need, how the need will be met and measured, and financial information to include.

Fundraising & support raising

In order to resource your ministry, you must be able to provide current and potential ministry partners with two things:

  • A strategic plan with quantifiable goals
  • A budget based on the plan Once you’ve established the plan and the financial resources you’ll need to work the plan, you’ll need to develop some communication pieces to present your need, including a proposal.

Your partners don’t expect an MBA level business plan, but they will expect your proposal to be

  • Concise
  • Clear
  • Compelling A first step in communicating needs is the Executive Summary. This is a single page document that can be used as a cover page or an introduction before presenting a more in-depth plan. Use the following worksheet to develop content for an Executive Summary.

    1. ORGANIZATIONAL BACKGROUND
  • a) Track record – how have you been successful in accomplishing you mission?
  • b) Leadership – brief bios of your leadership team.
  • c) Programs – in what ways do you accomplish your mission?
  • d) Partnerships – how do you partner with other organizations and churches to accomplish your; strategy?
    1. CURRENT NEED INCLUDING:
  • a) What are the social, political and/or financial conditions creating or contributing to this need?
  • b) Who are the beneficiaries and what are their specific needs?
    1. MEETING THE NEED AND MEASURING IMPACT:
  • a) What is the overall goal of this request?
  • b) What are the measurable objectives including time frame for accomplishing each objective for this request?
  • c) What is the overall outcome you hope to see as a result of this request?
  • d) How are you different from other organizations meeting this need?

  • c) What sources of funding have been requested for this project?
  • d) If this is an ongoing project, describe your plan for future funding?
