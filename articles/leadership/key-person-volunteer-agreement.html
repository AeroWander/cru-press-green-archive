Key Person Volunteer Agreement

320 words · 1 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A Key Person Volunteer Agreement form in which a campus ministry volunteer commits to spiritual disciplines, ongoing accountability, evangelism, and a life of moral integrity (no alcohol/tobacco use around students, sexual purity), acknowledging their status as an uncompensated volunteer under staff supervision.

Launching a new ministry

Campus Crusade for Christ

I understand the mission of Campus Crusade for Christ is to “Turn Lost Students into Christ-Centered Laborers.” As a volunteer I commit to pursue the following:

A Heart for God

  • By submitting to the Lordship of Christ in my daily life and ministry.
  • By spending consistent time in the Word and prayer in an ongoing relationship with Christ.
  • By being authentic in my relationship with God & others.
  • By agreeing with and living by the Statement of Faith.

A Heart for Growth

  • By communicating monthly with the Volunteer Coordinator or my staff contact for direction, training and accountability.
  • By attending a local church to receive insight and encouragement.
  • By being committed to consider & pray about attending retreats and conferences.
  • Attending times set aside for volunteer training or making arrangements to receive the training at other times if I can not make it.

A Heart for Evangelism

  • By taking part in the Great Commission (Matt 28:18-20) and making disciples. A Life of Integrity
  • By not drinking alcoholic beverages or using tobacco with or around students.
  • By living a sexually pure life according to the Scriptures.
  • By agreeing to keep with the doctrinal statement, mission, vision and distinctive of Crusade while teaching and training.
  • By understanding that I will be held accountable by Campus Crusade for Christ staff and would be asked to step down in the event of purity, emotional or practical concerns. Additionally, I acknowledge that my relationship with Campus Crusade for Christ is that of an uncompensated volunteer. I will use my best efforts to fulfill my responsibilities in support of Campus Crusade for Christ’s philosophy, methodology, and theology, which has as its goal helping reach the world with the Gospel of Christ. I understand that my activities will be supervised by a Campus Crusade for Christ staff member, who has final authority.

Date ____________ Name _______________________________________ Signature ____________________________________ Volunteer Assignment_________________________________________________________
