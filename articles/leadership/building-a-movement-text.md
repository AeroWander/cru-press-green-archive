MAIN CATEGORY: LAUNCHING A NEW MINISTRY

Sub-Category Heading: Building a Movement

Sub-Category Text:

The ingredients that lead to a growing movement are not complicated as long as you remember that when to do things is as important as what things to do. This is why the resources in this section try to follow a chronological order. This can get tricky when activities overlap, but there is room in the order and timing for flexibility. The order of activities that are suggested here provide a helpful structure from which you may choose to deviate.

File: Leadership Retreats.pdf

Step 6: Planning Part 1

File: Planning Part 1.pdf

Description: As you plan for the year, and bring others into that planning at the leadership retreats, it is important to map out both ‘what’ you are going to do and ‘how’ you are going to do it.

Tags: Movement Building, Planning, Preparation, Leadership Retreat, Semester

Step 6.5: Planning Part 2

File: Planning Part 2.pdf

Description: With the ‘what’ and the ‘how’ mapped out, factor into your planning the ‘when’ as well. Different times of the year are better for different ministry objectives.

Step 7: The First Few Weeks

File: The First Few Weeks.pdf

Description: Weeks 1–3. The first few weeks offer a unique opportunity that will not be duplicated during any other time of the school year. What is accomplished in these weeks will set the trajectory for the entire year.

Step 8: 30-Second Surveys

File: 30-Second Surveys.pdf

Description: Weeks 1–3. Perhaps the most significant activity of these first few weeks is the Dinner-line Survey. It allows you to gather hundreds, even thousands of filtered contacts.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Gathering, Surveys, Dinner-line, Freshman

Step 9: Publicity

File: Publicity.pdf

Description: Weeks 1–3. Posters, flyers, surveys, rent a blimp if you need to but use every avenue of promotion available to make your ministry as visible as possible in the first few weeks on campus.

Tags: Movement Building, First Weeks, Gathering, Publicity, Posters, Advertising

Step 10: Gathering Bible Studies

File: Gathering Studies.pdf

Description: Weeks 1–3. Gathering Bible Studies provide a critical holding-tank for students wanting to get involved. They provide an event you can publicize and invite-to, while postponing the thoughtful process of placing people in the ‘right’ Bible Study.

Step 11: The Men’s and Women’s Overnight

File: Men's and Women's.pdf

Description: Weeks 4–6. Having a Men’s/Women’s overnight between the start of the semester and the Fall Retreat provides a stutter-step of involvement: an opportunity to get to know people better.

Step 12: Launch Small Groups

File: Small Group Structure.pdf

Description: Weeks 4-6. Having gathered as many new people as possible, you now want to place them in a Bible study that will be best suited to their commitment and maturity level. This explains a way to structure your Small Groups.

Step 13: Establish Discipleship Relationships

File: Discipleship.pdf

Description: Weeks 4–6. Discipleship, like all mentoring, is an intensive investment of time and energy. Good stewardship demands selectivity, and as a leader it is important to give consideration to who should receive such an investment.

Step 14: The Fall Retreat

File: All About the Fall Retreat.pdf

Description: Congratulations, you made it. The past weeks of ministry have been one long sprint to the Fall Retreat. The Fall Retreat is not simply one more event. It is the event that consolidates all of your labor up to this point.

Step 15: Time For An Outreach

File: Time For Outreach.pdf

Step 17: Socials and Community Building

File: Socials.pdf

Description: Late November–Early December. This time of year lends itself to holding some creative socials, holiday parties, and other special times of community building. But don’t forget the other focus of this time: recruiting to Christmas Conference.

Step 18: The Christmas Conference

File: The Christmas Conference.pdf

Description: The Christmas Conference accelerates the growth and commitment of students, solidifies student leadership, and students return to campus ready and eager to lead, serve, and reach the campus for Christ.

Tags: Movement Building, Christmas Conference, Challenge Recruiting

File: Second Semester Rally.pdf

Description: Late January–Early February. Your job at the start of the semester is to rally the ministry and bring them together as a committed community, “standing firm in one spirit, contending as one man for the faith of the gospel” (Phil. 1:27).

File: Outreach.pdf

Step 21: Big Break
