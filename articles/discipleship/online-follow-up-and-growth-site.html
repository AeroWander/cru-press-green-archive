Online Follow-up and Growth Site

By Marilyn Adamson

190 words · 1 min read · Article · For new believers

In brief

A short promotional description of StartingWithGod.com, an online follow-up and discipleship site created by Marilyn Adamson (also behind Everystudent.com) offering articles, email Bible studies, and online chat to help new and young believers grow in their faith, for use standalone or alongside personal discipleship.

Discipleship & follow-up

Startingwithgod.com

Surfing the internet you’ll find thousands upon thousands of Christian websites but you’ll notice something strange: there is really no place you can send a new or young believer to ground them in the basics of the Christian faith and help them grow in their relationship with Christ.

Drawing from her experience in developing one of the most popular and fruitful Christian sites on the internet (Everystudent.com), Marilyn Adamson has launched a new site for on-line follow-up and discipleship of new and young believers.

The evangelistic ease and opportunity of sending someone to everystudent.com to explore the gospel is now available for discipleship.

At StartingWithGod.com a young believer can find helpful articles dealing with foundational truths and principles that will ground them in their relationship with God. They can sign up for e-mail studies of the Scripture, engage in online chats, learn how to share their faith, and ask any and all questions that arise as they pursue their walk with God.

As a stand-alone strategy or simply to augment personal discipleship StartingWithGod.com is an essential tool for anyone seeking to make disciples. To explore the site go to: StartingWithGod.com
