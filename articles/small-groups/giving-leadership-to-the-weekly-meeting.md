## student linc

How the Weekly Meeting Team Fits into the Big Picture: The weekly meeting is the movement’s opportunity to present itself to the campus. It must demonstrate the Mission, Vision and Values of Campus Crusade for Christ. It needs to balance between being inviting to the non-Christian and encouraging to the Christian. Although it is not the movement, it is the face of the movement. It is an opportunity to teach, align and encourage the movement and present the gospel to the campus.
