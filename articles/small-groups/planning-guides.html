Student Linc Bible Study & Discipleship Planner

Planning is an essential ingredient of an effective Bible study and life-changing discipleship. Planning matters. These sheets provide a place for you to map out the semester so that one weeks flows to the next, and so that you don’t get to the end of the semester and realize that major topics and issues went unaddressed.
