### surrendered to God.” D.L. Moody

Surrender. Easy to say, harder to do. It’s a word that makes most people feel uncomfortable. It means relinquishing control and entrusting yourself to someone else.

We have two choices. We will give ourselves fully to this world, running after its pleasures and “bling”, letting others dictate to us what is right and good and fulfilling, and asking ourselves each morning as we hurry off to work… is this really the abundant life?

Consider the second option – we surrender ourselves to Jesus. We believe God truly loves us (Jn 3.16). That His intentions towards us are kind and good (Eph 1.9), and that He plans to give us a future and a hope (Jer 29.11). We were made for this. Most Christians don’t purposely walk away from God; rather, they drift away. We miss the abundant life Jesus promised and simply settle for “good enough”. We choose to hold tightly to our wants and desires, our time and treasures, unwilling to release it all to Christ not realizing by hording our lives, we lose it all (Mt 10:38–39).

The choice is all yours. Our good and giving God is raising up a new generation of men and women to follow Him fully. Has God put His hand on your life for “such a time as this”?

### Kingdom Vision

The movement you were part of on campus and the vision you had for making a kingdom impact there were vital. They helped you fulfill God’s purposes for you. Where will you find that same environment next?

### Plan

If you aim at nothing you’ll hit it every time. Unless you’re a trained chef or just incredibly gifted, most people don’t walk into the kitchen and whip up a gourmet meal without a recipe. It doesn’t have to be elaborate, but the plan should have simple, doable steps that will help you begin to implement your God-given vision. What are your first three steps to bring about this vision for God’s Kingdom?

### Things Team

Without a compelling community, few people can survive for long on their own. Ecclesiastes 4 says, “It’s better to have a partner than go it alone. Share the work… and if one falls down, the other helps.”

Who shares your heart, in that they, too, are surrendered to Jesus and have a vision for your industry or neighborhood?

### Ongoing Equipping

Just as teachers, pilots and doctors need continuing education, so you will need ongoing equipping in order to make your best contribution to the kingdom.

### Coach

Proverbs stresses the importance of gaining wisdom from others. We can never underestimate the blessing of another who has gone before us and learned a thing or two along the way. A coach is just that person: someone to answer your questions, help you when you might be stuck, or encourage you when the going gets tough. If there is someone already out there doing what you have a vision for? Can you join them?
