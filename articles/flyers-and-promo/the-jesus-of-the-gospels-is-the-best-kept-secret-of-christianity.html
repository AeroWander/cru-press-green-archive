The Jesus of the Gospels Is the Best-kept Secret of Christianity

37 words · 1 min read · Flyer · For students

In brief

A Cru Bible study invitation flyer asking what Jesus was really like, contrasting him with pop-culture figures (Mr. Rogers, Spock, John Lennon, Santa Claus), and inviting students to a discussion (date, time, and place left blank to fill in).
