Wouldn’t it be great to have all the answers to the essential aspects of life? What if God were to give you all the answers — and give you the freedom to take his advice or not? That’s just what he’s done in revealing his thoughts, his perspective and himself in the Bible. He not only gives you the answers, but he personally speaks to your life through it. If you’d like to investigate the Bible with others, please join us. To check out the answers for yourself, read the section called “John.” Life’s test is open book.
