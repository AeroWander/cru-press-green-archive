100 words · 1 min read · Handout · For students

In brief

A short wordplay-style promotional text for Cru built around "C U" (see you), asserting that God knows and loves the reader personally and sacrificed himself for them, closing with the tagline "Real Community, Real Faith." Appears to be draft flyer or ad copy rather than a full article.
