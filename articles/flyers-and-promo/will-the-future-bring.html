Will the Future Bring?

78 words · 1 min read · Flyer · For seekers

In brief

An EveryStudent.com outreach flyer asking what the future holds and offering God as a reliable, unchanging source of peace and confidence amid uncertainty, pointing readers to the feature article "Peace of Mind in an Unstable World" on EveryStudent.com.

Evangelism toolsPersonal evangelism & gospel conversations

What

will the future bring?

No matter what happens, in the world or in your personal life, what can you draw on to face it with confidence and hope? As your world changes, is there a reliable constant?

Fortunately, we have a tremendous resource: a strong God who never changes…is always reliable, there for us…offers his peace to keep us together when circumstances seem to be falling apart…and is willing to engage in the details of our lives. Find out more...

“Peace of Mind in an Unstable World”
