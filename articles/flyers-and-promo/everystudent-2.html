EveryStudent 2

228 words · 1 min read · Flyer · For seekers

In brief

A Christmas-themed EveryStudent.com evangelistic flyer inviting readers to let Jesus into their lives just as he was born into a humble stable, quoting Revelation 3:20 and encouraging readers to pray a simple invitation and begin reading the Gospel of John, with a pointer to EveryStudent.com for more.

Evangelism toolsPersonal evangelism & gospel conversationsAssurance, grace & forgiveness

This Christmas This Christmas Jesus Is Looking for a Jesus Is Looking for a New Stable to Dwell In. New Stable to Dwell In. Your Place Would Be Perfect. Your Place Would Be Perfect.

It is no small miracle that the God of the universe was willing to leave heaven and be born in a lowly, obscure stable in a Middle Eastern town. But perhaps an even greater wonder is that this same God is willing to come into the dirt and filth of our lives. Why? To bring healing, forgiveness and wholeness. He’s not waiting for you to clean up your stable or to make your life more acceptable to him. Instead, he wants to come into your life now and establish a relationship with you.

Jesus said, “Behold, I stand at the door and knock; if anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him...”

This is what it takes to begin a relationship with God. Not perfect, holy living. A simple yet sincere invitation, and Jesus said he will enter your life. Pretty amazing. You can invite him into your heart right now. Then open to the section called “John” in the Bible and begin reading. You’ll see what kind of life he has for you. If you have some questions or want to explore this more, go to EveryStudent.com.
