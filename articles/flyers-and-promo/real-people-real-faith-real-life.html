Real People, Real Faith, Real Life

44 words · 1 min read · Flyer · For students

In brief

A promotional flyer for Cru's weekly meeting, describing Cru as a community of "real faith, real people, real life" open to Christians and non-Christians alike, including those with doubts or questions, with blanks for time, date, and place.

Weekly meetings

Cru is a community where the gospel is lived as well as taught—real faith, real people, real life. But it’s not just for Christians: it’s also a place to bring your honest questions, problems, and doubts. Hope to see you at our Weekly Meeting.
