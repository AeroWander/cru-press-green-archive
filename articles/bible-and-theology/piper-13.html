Piper 13

226 words · 1 min read · Handout · For small-group leaders

In brief

Small-group discussion questions for chapter 13 of Piper's Seeing and Savoring Jesus Christ, on the second coming of Christ in glory. Questions ask participants what they long for most intensely and challenge them to consider whether they anticipate Christ's return with similar longing, and what is uniquely beautiful about Christ appearing as judge and restorer.

Bible study & interpretationKnowing God & doctrine

The Appearing of the Glory of our Great Savior

Piper 13

1.What’s the most soul-gripping thing you’ve ever anticipated . . . some thing, some person, or some event that you LONGED for? Tell us about it.

  1. Do you LONG for the appearing of Christ with the same soul-gripping intensity that you long for other things? If not, why not?

[there are a host of possible good discussions that could flow from this – e.g.: I don’t really love him – so why long for him? I love other things like the day I graduate from Medical School way more than him; I never think about him or that day – I’m too focused on the next test; Maybe the person has never been taught or thought about that day. Maybe the person doesn’t know Christ, etc. Maybe the person is so enamored with the present world that a change doesn’t seem that beautiful, etc.]

  1. What is uniquely beautiful about a Christ who returns a second time in power to judge and restore? Clearly Christ is beautiful beckoning the children; He is beautiful suffering on the cross; He is beautiful calming the seas. BUT what do we see of Him in His final appearance that is unique? What is it about Christ in this act that wows your soul and draws you to him?
