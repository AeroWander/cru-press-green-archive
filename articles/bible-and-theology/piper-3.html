Small-group discussion questions for chapter 3 of Piper's Seeing and Savoring Jesus Christ, on the 'diverse excellencies' of Christ as both slain Lamb and conquering King. Questions explore the imagery of Christ as the worthy Lamb in Revelation 5 and believers as the Lamb's bride in Revelation 19.

The Diverse Excellencies of Jesus Christ

Piper, Chapter 3

“The glory of Christ is not a simple thing” (p. 36). In fact, if we make Christ simple we diminish his glory. For example, his mercy is all-the-more-beautiful in light of his wrath. Surely the scene of Jesus weeping at Lazarus’ tomb is more poignant because Jesus volitionally chose to enter this world of sorrows.

  1. In Rev 5 an angel asks, “Who is worthy to open the scroll?” At first no one in heaven or earth is found who is worthy, so John weeps. But then an elder says, “WEEP NO MORE” – someone is worthy. He is the Lamb (5:6). He is slain (5:6). And then they sing, “Worthy is the Lamb who was slain.”

Why is a slain Lamb who conquered so beautiful and worthy of adoration?

  1. Later in Revelation another “diverse excellency” is seen in the Lamb metaphor: One of the angels tells John to “come, I will show you the Bride, the wife of the Lamb” (19:9). God’s people are described as “the wife” of the Lamb.

The slain lamb who conquered has invited you, and made you, his WIFE.

How does your heart respond to being the WIFE of the Lamb?

  1. In what specific ways is a powerful, reigning, husband-Lamb presently balm for your soul?
