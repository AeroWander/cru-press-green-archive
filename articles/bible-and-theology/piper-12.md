A Stunning Display of Glory: The Resurrection

Piper, 12

1. From a spectator’s standpoint, events hadn’t gone well for Jesus. He’d been wrongly accused. His disciples had bailed. And now all could see him, hanging dead on a tree.

Little did spectators know that all of this was simply a prelude.

Then he rose from the dead.

Imagine the thrill of seeing Jesus rise from the dead. How could a heart stand it! Ponder it. If you were there, what do you think your heart might have experienced at that moment.

2. Natural wonders can be awe-inspiring, like when Mount St. Helens erupted, blowing away the entire side of a mountain. But how awe-inspiring is the conquest of DEATH?

Jesus’ raw power is stunning in his defeat of death.

What is so uniquely beautiful about a Jesus that conquers Satan and death?

3. Jesus’ defeat of death did more than conquer death. It assures you that death has no hold on you. It assures you of your ultimate victory.

How would your life be different if you lived each moment fully conscious of your intimate union with the One who conquered death?
