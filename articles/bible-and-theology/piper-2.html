188 words · 1 min read · Handout · For small-group leaders

In brief

Small-group discussion questions for chapter 2 of Piper's Seeing and Savoring Jesus Christ, on Christ as eternally and uniquely glorious. Questions ask whether Christ functions as an 'end' or a 'means' in the participant's life and reflect on Jesus' self-existence as the Alpha and Omega (Revelation 22).

Bible study & interpretationKnowing God & doctrine

Jesus: Always Uniquely Glorious

Piper, Chapter 2

  1. Piper states “Christ does not exist in order to make much of us. We exist in order to enjoy making much of him” (p. 27).

Think of your relationship with Christ. Is Christ most often an “end” – the object of your enthrallment and interest? Or is Christ a “means” – someone who is pursued to get you something else? Why is this the case?

  1. In Isaiah 44:6 God boasts, “Thus says the LORD, the King of Israel and his Redeemer, the LORD of hosts: I am the first and I am the last, and there is no God besides Me.”

Jesus states that he, too, preceded everything and will endure far after all things: “Behold, I am coming quickly . . . I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end” (Rev 22:12-13,16).

How is Jesus uniquely beautiful because he “was” before there was anything else?

How can one’s soul find a unique kind of solace in a relationship with one who preceded – and will endure beyond—all?
