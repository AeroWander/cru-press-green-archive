The Great Mercies of Christ

Piper 10

1. Rich people sometimes give of their riches, like the couple that just gave $25 million to Duke. In our culture we general use “rich” for money (or chocolate).

Yet in the Bible we often see God’s “riches” as tied to something else other than possessions or money. For example, Paul knows that God is rich -- he owns all things

But in Romans 2:4 Paul specifically mentions God’s riches as what?

Do you show contempt or gratitude for this aspect of God’s riches?

2. Compare these two statements:

1. The father gave a wedding banquet for his only daughter

2. The father lavished his riches upon his only daughter at her wedding banquet.

What does the phrase “lavished his riches” communicate that the verb “gave” doesn’t?

Read Eph 1:7.

What has God lavished on you?

How?

How does your heart respond to this word “lavish”?

3. Read Romans 15:9. What would your life be like if your heart was continually “glorifying God for his mercy”?
