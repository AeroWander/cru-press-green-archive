It’s not all about you

Piper, Chapter 1

What is at the root of what is wrong in our world?

What is the meaning of the vastness of the universe?

Have you ever had an experience of God’s greatness like Isaiah describes in 40:26?

According to Piper, what is the deepest longing (of the greatest ache) of the human heart?

How is out heart supposed to respond to the beauty of creation?

How is there healing in beholding splendor?

According to Piper, what is the gospel about? How do we subtly distort it?

What was the highest good Jesus could do for us? John 17:24

What is the aim of the gospel? (that we could see and savor the glory of Christ)

Tell us about something you really savor. What (who?, where?) is it? How does it affect you? Tell us about the savoring . . . the longing, the sweetness. Be specific.

Now let me ask you a question: Have you ever savored Jesus Christ? Why do you suppose this is the case? How does your heart respond to the possibility of “savoring” Jesus Christ?

According to Piper, what is the deepest longing (of the greatest ache) of the human heart?

How do we attempt to satisfy our hunger apart from Christ?

But how, one might ask, do we do place the glory of God at the center – and consequently ease our hunger? Paul tells us in 2 Corinthians 4:6:

“God, who said ‘Light shall shine out of darkness,’ is the One who has shone in our hearts to give the Light of the glory of God IN THE FACE OF CHRIST.”

When we look in the face of Christ we see the glory of God.

What (or who) can be found “in the face of Christ” that can heal the aches of your heart and satisfy the hunger in your soul?

Or maybe put another way: What is beautiful about the glory of God in the face of Christ that far surpasses the “beauty” of any self-devised plan to satisfy your hurting heart?

The next few weeks we’ll fill our eyes with the full portrait of Jesus as provided in the Bible – seeing Him for who he is, savoring him for who he is.
