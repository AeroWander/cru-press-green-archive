Proving Jesus More-to-be-Desired

Piper, Chapter 9

Piper tackles a difficult question in this chapter: Why hasn’t Christ removed Satan and all evil when he could? He concludes that Christ must have something more to demonstrate than just his power over Satan.

What he wants to demonstrate, according to Piper, is that he is not only stronger: he is infinitely more to be desired. Millions of saints now turn their back on Satan because of the greater glory and grace of Christ, thus proving Christ more desirable and displaying his glory in a unique way. When we make much of Jesus, we thus shame Satan(p. 85-86).
