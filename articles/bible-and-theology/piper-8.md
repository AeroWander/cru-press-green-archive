The Incomparable Sufferings

Piper Chapter 8

1. Throughout history real people – real moms, dads, sons, and daughters – have been subjected to inhumane torture and suffering. Their stories are too overwhelming to bear; our human minds struggle to block them out as a bad dream. [note, for example, the recent beheading of Daniel Pearl, who left behind a wife pregnant with their first child]

As we read the biblical accounts we rightly recoil from the grossness of Jesus’ suffering, just as we cringe when we hear of Daniel Pearl’s fate.

What, however, is uniquely amazing about Jesus’ sufferings that makes his anguish unlike any other anguish in the history of humanity?

2. If Jesus could have paid for your sins in a court room, by paying a fine, what aspects of his beautiful person would you have missed that are made so much more evident by his anguish and death?

3. Two of the more amazing statements made by Jesus regarding his death are found in John 10:18 and John 15:13. He fully controls the situation and lays down his life for his friends. How does your heart respond to the one who did this for you?
