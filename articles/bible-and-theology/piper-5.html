In brief

Small-group discussion questions for chapter 5 of Piper's Seeing and Savoring Jesus Christ, on Jesus calming the storm in Mark 4 as evidence of his authority over nature. Questions probe whether Christ's absolute power is comforting or unsettling and why the disciples grew more afraid after the storm ended.

The Winds and the Waves Still Know His Voice

Piper Chapter 5

Piper entitled this chapter “The Winds and the Waves Still Know His Voice,” presumably because they knew his voice in Mark 4 and Piper is reminding us of this fact: they still know his voice.

But let’s change the title slightly – definitely within Piper’s thought – to:

The Winds and the Waves Obey Him.

There is no Mother Nature. He speaks and it happens.

  1. Is this fact comforting or disturbing to you?
  1. In Mark 4 the disciples in the boat were TERRIFIED, rightfully fearing for their lives. After the seas calmed down they had an “a ha” moment, but it isn’t what you would expect. In fact, Mark tells us that AFTER the seas were calm “they were filled with great fear.”

Why were they FILLED with GREAT FEAR after the storm?

  1. What does it say about your Christ if you aren’t terrified of him?
  1. How is Christ more beautiful because the winds and the waves obey him?
