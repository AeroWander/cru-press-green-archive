In brief

A short outline handout presenting a four-step Bible study framework: Survey (historical, literary, redemptive context), Explore (careful textual observation suited to genre), Discover (summarize the passage's big idea), and Respond (apply the passage in light of Christ). Includes brief bullet-point steps for each stage.
