Bible Overview Handouts for God s Big Picture

By Vaughan Roberts

1,391 words · 6 min read · Handout

In brief

Teaching handouts for a Bible overview series (based on Vaughan Roberts' 'God's Big Picture'/'Gospel and Kingdom' framework) tracing the storyline of Scripture as the kingdom of God through eight stages, from creation and the fall through Israel's history, the prophets, Christ's coming, and the church age. Includes outline notes, key Bible references, discussion blanks, and application questions for each session.
