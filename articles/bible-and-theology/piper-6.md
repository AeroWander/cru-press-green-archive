Something Greater Than Solomon is Here

Piper Chapter 6

Read John 16:29-30

1. Think for a moment on the reality that Jesus knows your every thought, emotion, intention and private action.

Does this comfort you? Terrify you? Shock you?

2. What are some reasonable responses –attitudes, emotions, and actions – to a Jesus who knows all?

3. How do you need to change in light of a Jesus who knows all?
