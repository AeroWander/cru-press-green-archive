Gospel of John Study Over Email

282 words · 1 min read · Article · For new believers, seekers

In brief

A brief promotional description of a free email Bible study on the Gospel of John offered through StartingWithGod.com, aimed at new believers and seekers. Explains that John was written to prove Jesus is the Son of God and that the study covers Jesus's life, foundational truths about relationship with God, and basic Bible study skills.

Bible study & interpretationDiscipleship & follow-up

Startingwithgod.com

The Bible study e-mails can be discontinued at anytime. Just unsubscribe at the bottom of any email. Email addresses are never sold or shared with anyone. Ever.

To keep e-mails compast and easy to read through, this study of the Gospel of John will not be exhaustive – not every sentence or even every chapter. But rest assured all of the major themese and applicable teachings will be covered.

A special offer to Know God Better – a study in the Gospel of John. This is a free Bible study in the Gospel of John, sent by email.

You can sign up for the Gospel of John study by going to: StartingWithGod.com If you’ve never read or studied the Bible, people often recommend starting with the Gospel of John. The reason is simple. John was written primarily to new believers and seekers. The Gospel of John is loaded with truths about God and how he views our relationship with him.

The book of John was written to prove that Jesus Christ is the Son of God. As an eyewitness to the love and power displayed in the miracles of Jesus, John gives us an up-close and personal look at Christ’s identity. He shows us that Jesus, though fully God, came in the flesh to distinctly and accurately reveal God, and that Christ is the source of eternal life to all who believe in him.

The goal of the study is three-fold:

  1. To look at Jesus’ life and what he said.
  2. To understand foundational truths about our relationship with God.
  3. To learn how to read and study the Bible on your own – useful for the rest of your life.
