Student Venture

255 words · 1 min read · Article

In brief

A short flyer introducing Student Venture, Campus Crusade for Christ's junior high and high school ministry since 1966, inviting readers to find or register a ministry at their school, volunteer, or learn more at gocampus.org, and stressing the value of partnering with students, parents, teachers, and coaches.

High School Ministry

Gocampus.org

Student Venture is the high school and junior high ministry of Campus Crusade for Christ. Since 1966, we have been reaching out to teenagers with more than 1,300 full-time staff members, Student Venture affiliates, and local community volunteers.

We are a community of Spirit-led, Christ-centered, spiritually maturing believers who are like-minded in mission and purpose. We are only effective when we are collectively engaging the heart and mind of our Lord Jesus Christ. Without the vitality of a rich intimate relationship with God and firm grasp of His infallible Word, our missional framework is meaningless.

Launching an Sv Ministry

Ministry is more fun and effective as you work with others. It’s important that you get connected with people in your community to help your ministry develop. Whether you are involving students that will be part of your team, parents who will help support your ministry, teachers and youth pastors who know students or talking to a Student Venture Coaching Center coach, everyone can help your ministry to grow so more students can hear about Jesus.

  • Find others at your school doing ministry.
  • We’re here for you! Call us at 1.877.GOCAMPUS or find out how you can volunteer with Student Venture here.
  • Register yourself at your school so that others can find you
  • Apply to work with Student Venture

To find out more information about Student Venture or to launching a movement among Middle School or High School students go to: www.gocampus.org
