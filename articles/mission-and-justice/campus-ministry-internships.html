Campus Crusade for Christ has a rich history of students believing God for great things. College students have been taking one year STINTs and launching new campus ministries all over the world in places such as Russia, East Asia, and the Middle East. College students reaching college students–that’s the history of Campus Crusade for Christ.

And that history continues to be made. Right now on campuses all over the world students are ready to respond to the gospel. Yet, more laborers–students and those fresh out of college–are needed. God is calling people who desire to pioneer new ministries in the United States and around the world.

That’s why the Campus Ministry of Campus Crusade for Christ has developed this unique avenue of ministry. You could be a Campus Ministry intern–a one-year commitment to believe God to do something powerful in and through your life. We’ve been sending students for a one-year STINT overseas for years, and now we’re ready to send students everywhere–both in the United States and internationally.

Perhaps you’re not sure what you’ll do with the rest of your life. Perhaps you would like to give a year of your lifeto reaching students, discipling leaders, and establishing new student ministries. Or maybe you can picture yourself using your skills in your field of study for ministry. Maybe you would just like to spend a year getting a feel for full-time ministry.

What can happen in a year? In just a year, you could pioneer a new ministry on a campus overseas. In just a year, you could open up new campus ministries in the United States. In just a year, you could establish a ministry to reach ethnic students on a campus.

And why go alone? Bring your friends with you! Ministry is much more effective when it’s done by teams of people believing God and working together.

Look through history. Thousands have gone before us to reach students. Now it’s our turn. It’s our turn to be the new pioneers who build the foundations for the next century.

What Is a Campus Ministry Internship?

Whether you are stateside or overseas, you will spend a year helping to fulfill the Great Commission. Use your gifts, abilities, and even professional skills to reach students for Christ.

As an Intern you will:

  • Receive training in the context of a team
  • Acquire skills to help you continue to minister in the marketplace
  • Gain time to decide if God may be leading you to full-time ministry
  • Make an eternal difference in the lives of students

Salary Upon acceptance as a Campus Ministry intern, you will have the opportunity and responsibility to raise your own financial support* to cover your salary, ministry expenses and medical coverage. Your actual amount will be determined by the location of your ministry assignment and the nature of your work in that assignment. You will receive further training on how to raise support for your ministry.

Note: A special funding opportunity exists for Impact interns. For more information, contact the Ethnic

Student Ministries national office at (407) 826-2581.

College Credit Your internship may qualify you for college credit through your university. Contact your university for more information.

Deadlines The deadline for submitting your completed application is March 1 st. The completed application includes your application, biographical material, all elaboration forms and questionnaires, all references, your signature page, and application fee. Late applications will be charged a $50 non-refundable application fee. Absolutely no applications will be received after April 1st.

Internship Opportunities

Catalytic Catalytic is the umbrella term for mobilizing a growing coalition of staff members, interns, students, churches, and volunteers to expand to new, unreached campuses. Picture yourself as part of a Metro Team in a major city like New York, Atlanta, San Francisco or Chicago. Or imagine being part of a pioneering team opening new campus ministries for a broader geographic area like Virginia, Wisconsin or Oregon. Students are open, and we need your help to bring the gospel to every campus

Ethnic Student Ministries (ESM)

Working with ESM, you can help reach a large and vital population of college students. You could be part of the Impact movement to touch African-American students with the gospel. You could reach Hispanic-American college students through the Destino movement. Or join a team reaching Asian-Americans through the Epic movement. Come and be a part of this growing movement of students, volunteers and church leaders who are doing God’s work in innovative ways, all to ensure that every student hears the gospel.

More information on ESM internships can be found at each movement’s web site:

The Impact Fellowship Program The Epic Internship The Destino Internship

Staffed Campuses

How many U.S. college students are on God’s heart? All 17 million of them! You can be a part of what God is doing on campuses in the United States by working with a full-time Campus Ministry staff team. You can help create new strategies, gain leadership experience, hone your small group skills, and invest yourself in something that will outlive you. Imagine the impact one year will have on the rest of your life

Worldwide Student Network (WSN)

Imagine yourself sitting in a café in Florence, walking the streets of Argentina, or perusing a city market in the Middle East while getting to know university students and talking with them about your relationship with Christ. You have the opportunity to take the life-changing message of Christ to students around the world. You’ll be amazed at the similarities you find as you engage in conversation about your lives, and gripped by the spiritual need of the students you meet.

Bridges International The world has come to our door. Some 700,000 of the world’s brightest students are studying and living in the United States. How would you like to spend a year helping build a movement on a campus or in a metropolitan area that is reaching these strategic international students with the gospel and mobilizing them for the worldwide harvest?

Many of tomorrow’s world leaders are on U.S. campuses today, often coming from nations that are difficult for Americans to enter. As a Bridges International intern, you will have the chance to emphasize ministry with one or more of the following people group networks: Chinese, Japanese, or “The Ishmael Project”. God has brought the world’s future leaders to us. We need to send them home with more than an academic education. For more information contact Scott Garrity at 734-347-2001 or Scott. Garrity@uscm.org

National Campus Office and Campus Ministry Regional Offices Use your skills in accounting, computer technology and programming, graphic design, video, journalism, marketing, communication, advertising and many others to help reach students. For a vital worldwide movement, it’s crucial to have qualified individuals doing ministry in highly skilled positions. Consider being an intern in a Campus Ministry regional office or at the nerve center of Campus Crusade for Christ– the international headquarters in Orlando, Florida.

In ten cities around the nation staff members focus on the leadership and administrative needs to move our mission forward. The regional office might be a great place for you to serve if you have gifts or interest in accounting, computer technology and programming, graphic design, video, journalism, marketing, communication, advertising or other business skills.

For more information on Campus Ministry internships and instructions on how to apply, go to the Campus Ministry Website.
