Greek Movement

252 words · 1 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A short overview of Cru's Greek Movement, a ministry to fraternity and sorority members, describing its vision of every Greek house having a spiritual legacy and its three-fold mission to pioneer resources, promote a leader network, and partner with Greek organizations, with contact information for starting a local chapter.

Growing Greeks

Greekmovement.com

Greek Movement is a movement of Campus Crusade for Christ. We exist to inspire and equip every fraternity and sorority member to leave a spiritual legacy in thier Greek House. We also desire to serve the Greek community by offering resources on leadership, spiritual growth, and Greek life and culture.

Our Vision

That the world would be transformed by the next generation of fraternity and sorority leaders coming out of the college campus and into the larger society.

Give Every Greek in Every House the opportunity to leave a spiritual legacy in their fraternity or sorority.

3-Fold Mission

Pioneer: new ideas and tools to equip fraternity and sorority students to lead more passionately and effectively.

Promote: a network of student and alumni leaders across the country to share ideas, concerns, tips and experiences with one another—which would ultimately enhance Greek life on a national level.

Partner: with national fraternity and sorority leadership, as well as campus Greek advisers, to help serve them by mentoring/volunteering on a local level .

Starting a Greek Ministry

Greekmovement.com is designed to help interested individuals that want to help launch a ministry to fraternities and sororities on their campus. Our staff can give you resources such as Bible study materials and let you know about conferences for Greek students. We really do want to help you grow in your faith and to have an impact on those around you.

If you have any questions, please e-mail: Greek@uscm.org
