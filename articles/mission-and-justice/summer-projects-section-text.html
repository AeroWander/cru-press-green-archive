Sub-Category Heading: Summer Projects / Staff / Internships

Sub-Category Text:

You’ll find resources here dealing with mission and ministry opportunities. Summer Projects, Campus Crusade Internships, Short-Term Missions, Campus Staff: options range from part-time to full-time and everywhere in-between, locations include just about anywhere on the planet. If you have a heart to serve we have a place for you to use it. For the most current updates on dates, places and application deadlines, go to the Campus Crusade Student Site.
