Ethnic & contextualized ministry

International

Reaching Internationals

The United States is home to approximately 750,000 international students and visiting scholars. It’s no stretch of the imagination to predict that many of the world’s next generation of business, political and academic leaders will emerge from this elite group.

What Is Bridges?

Bridges International began as a specialized ministry to international students from China in 1983 and was broadened to focus on all international students in 2000. We currently have 180 staff members and interns spread across US campuses. Students from some of the world’s hardest-to-reach places are here in our house. Our vision is to reach the nations here… that we might reach the nations there.

Reachinginternationals.com

Reachinginternationals.com is an effort to put some of the “best practices” for ministry to international students in one place. Those experienced and those new to the work have contributed ideas, tools, and strategies that are effective.

If you were to visit the locations where dynamic ministry is occurring among internationals you would usually find a strong network of partnering organizations that work and pray together toward a common goal. RI is no different. Links to all the major “ISM” ministries are included on this site as are the best practices and recommended resources from ISI, Intervarsity, Navigators, ChiAlpha, and Bridges Int’l to name a few.

If you would like information about becoming a volunteer with Bridges International call our main office in Austin, Texas, at (512) 250-5046. You can also email Michelle Drouin to get students connected for coaching: Michelle.Drouin@uscm.org
