Gospel in Action Campus Partners for Humanitarian and Social Justice Initiatives

Campus Ministry leadership and other staff members have connected with a number of partners that can help us engage with victims of natural disaster or with the “quartet of the vulnerable” – the widow, orphan, alien, and poor. These partners are Christ-centered, and each has a mission that is consistent with our mission of seeing lost students become Christ-centered laborers. They provide great opportunities for us to expose students and faculty to human need as we “win, build, and send” them into the world armed with the gospel of Jesus and with His love and power to help, heal, redeem, and restore.

TRANSFORMATIONAL COMMUNITY 1 Campus Ministry Partners for Humanitarian and Social Justice Initiatives Campus Ministry leadership and other staff members have connected with a number of partners that can help us engage with victims of natural disaster or with the “quartet of the vulnerable” – the widow, orphan, alien, and poor. These partners are Christ-centered, and each has a mission that is consistent with our mission of seeing lost students become Christ-centered laborers. They provide great opportunities for us to expose students and faculty to human need as we “win, build, and send” them into the world armed with the gospel of Jesus and with His love and power to help, heal, redeem, and restore.

The International Justice Mission (www.ijm.org) is a human rights agency that rescues victims of slavery, sexual exploitation and other forms of violent oppression. IJM seeks to make public justice systems work for victims of abuse and oppression who urgently need the protection of the law. By starting IJM chapters on our campuses, or connecting to one that already exists, we are helping free the enslaved by raising awareness and funds for the work of IJM, and we are sharing the light of the gospel in the context of the darkness of slavery, sexual exploitation, and violent oppression. For more information, read “Launching Justice Movements” at http://crupressgreen.com/launching-justice-movements/, or contact Libby.Swenson@cru.org.

The Christian Alliance for Orphans (www.christianalliancefororphans.org) unites more than 100 respected Christian organizations and a national network of churches. Working together, joint initiatives inspire, equip, and connect Christians to reflect God’s heart in caring for orphans in adoption, foster care and global orphan care initiatives. Family Life’s Hope for Orphans, and the Campus Ministry, are member organizations of the Alliance, and scores of Cru movements and summer projects have exposed students and faculty to orphans and their needs through partnering with Alliance member organizations. For a list of potential partners within the Alliance and their locations worldwide, see below. For more information, contact Dawn Little at Summer.Projects@cru.org, subject line: Dawn. The Campus Ministry’s on-going partnership with Haiti Cru and Nehemiah Vision Ministries provides a unique opportunity to help rebuild Haiti’s shattered campus ministry and to help build Chambrun (an area on the outskirts of Port-au-Prince) into a beacon of hope for some of the poorest people in the world. Opportunities include spring break trips, summer projects, or an entire semester in Haiti through Cru Global Missions’ new Cru Abroad program. For more information: http://gosummerproject.com/projects/680 or http://semesterinhaiti.com/, or contact Eric.Heistand@cru.org.

Here’s Life Inner City, Cru’s ministry to the urban poor in major cities of the USA, offers life-changing spring break opportunities (“Urban Immersion”) and summer opportunities (“Summer in the City”) as well as internships and career opportunities. New concern for the poor among college students compels hundreds of students annually to spend their spring break or summer with HLIC, where they are exposed to the needs of the city, grow personally, and are challenged to “win, build, and send” at their university in order to raise up Godly leadership for urban America. For details: http://www.seizeit.org/ Global Aid Network. As the relief and development aid arm of Cru USA and Campus Crusade for Christ International, the ministry of Global Aid Network (“GAiN”) spans the globe helping to meet physical needs in areas of poverty and despair and providing hope through evangelistic outreach. “GAiN” works in partnership with existing Cru ministries around the world to help distribute compassionate aid to impoverished countries and mobilizes mission teams for aid distribution, disaster response, evangelistic outreach, medical mission and orphan care. Go to: http://gainusa.org/ The Impact Movement led the way during the Campus Ministry’s response to Katrina, mobilizing 400 African American students to bring relief and hope to New Orleans and directing Cru efforts in the African American community there. If there are Impact movements near you and God is leading you to engage in predominantly African American schools or communities, contact Impact Movement leaders for direction and collaboration in these efforts. http://impactmovement.org/ Compassion International exists as a Christian child advocacy ministry that releases children from spiritual, economic, social and physical poverty and enables them to become responsible, fulfilled Christian adults. Founded in 1952, Compassion began providing Korean War orphans with food, shelter, education and health care, as well as Christian training. Today, Compassion helps more than 1.2 million children in 26 countries.

Compassion also has over 2000 university students involved their Leadership Development Program in the following countries: Bolivia, Brazil, Columbia, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Haiti, Honduras, India, Indonesia, Kenya, Peru, Philippines, Rwanda, Thailand, and Uganda. Check the website for ideas re: connecting with college students through sponsorship opportunities: http://www.compassion.com/, or with Jay.Lorenzen@cru.org re: involving LDP students in international partnership locations.

Local Cru movements and Big Break have partnered with Feed My Starving Children (http://www.fmsc.org/) and Feeding Children Everywhere (http:// www.feedingchildreneverywhere.com/) to pack high nutrition meals for starving children. Both are Christ-centered organizations that provide an opportunity for volunteers to pack high nutrition meals that are then shipped to hungry children all over the world. In 2011 and 2012, Cru and these two organizations packed and shipped over 3 million meals overseas. For an example of what this can look like on your campus, view 3-minute VDO at http:// www.youtube.com/watch? v=S5Drl5jKstA&list=UUeg4QHYNSqjhNRmWB9j21wQ&index=2&feature=plcp)

Vision Trust has a passion to love the most needy children in the world, and those who partner with Vision Trust will help provide food, medical care, schooling, and leadership development for the children, and will help disciple them into a generation of leaders who bring gospel-driven change to their society and culture. The Great Plains International region sponsors a summer project opportunity with Vision Trust in the Dominican Republic.

Students and faculty on this project engage with orphans, children in extreme poverty, or victims of trafficking. They also connect with the Cru summer project launching movements on university campuses in Santa Domingo. (Most of the college students involved in the DR Cru ministry have graduated from high school leadership development programs sponsored by Vision Trust.) For more info: http://www.gosummerproject.com/projects/790 In any partnership, we strive for a mutually beneficial relationship and a synergy that accelerates the growth of the Kingdom of God. All of the partners listed above (and others, of course) can help us connect Cru movements, summer projects, and Stint teams to human need within arms reach of the university – and we can give them lift in their sacrificial work in broken places. Once students and faculty are immersed in pressing human need, God will speak to them about how broken the world is, how desperately the gospel is needed, and how much we need to grow in order to bring the gospel and its power to redeem and restore the most broken places in the world. That’s a huge win for any Cru movement, and the enthusiasm of Cru students and staff often provide a big “shot in the arm” for sacrificial believers working in the trenches with the poor and oppressed.

If there’s an organization you’ve partnered with and would like to add them to this list, please contact Chip.Scivicque@cru.org.
