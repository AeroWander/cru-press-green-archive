MAIN CATEGORY: LAUNCHING A NEW MINISTRY

Sub-Category Heading: Ethnic/Contextualized Ministries

Sub-Category Text:

Every ethnic or affinity group on campus has unique characteristics that require cultural adaptations in the way we do ministry. Such adaptations are called contextualizing.

The Campus Ministry currently has contextualized ministries to the following ethnic communities: Asian Americans (Epic Movement); African Americans (Impact);

Native Americans (Nations); Internationals (Bridges); Latinos (Destino); and Koreans (KCCC).

In addition to these ethnic ministries, we have contextualized ministries to the following campus communities: Athletes (AIA); Greeks (Greek Movement); Military (Valor); and Faculty (Faculty Commons). Our ministry to middle and high school students is called Student Venture.
