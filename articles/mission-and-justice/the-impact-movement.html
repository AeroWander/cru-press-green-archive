The Impact Movement

376 words · 2 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

An overview of the Impact Movement, Cru's ministry to students of African descent, describing its vision of producing spiritually focused, financially responsible, morally fit Black leaders. It repeats the five steps for launching an Impact chapter (pray, decode the campus, find a person of peace, connect with an Impact coach, make yourself available to resource leaders) with contact information.

Ethnic & contextualized ministryLaunching a new ministry

Impact

Impactmovement.com

The Impact Movement takes the truth of Jesus Christ to the campus, community and world by producing leaders of African descent who are spiritually focused, financially responsible and morally fit.

The Impact Movement envisions each community of African descent fulfilling its destiny as a reflection of the redemptive power of Jesus Christ. We reach thousands of college students, lead those students in ministering to the community and expose those students to God’s work around the world. Our alumni continue to stay engaged through our Impact Association. I pray that this web site, our online resources and the various events we offer will be a great blessing to you.

To learn more about The Impact Movement, visit the Impact website and read more about launching an Impact movement by reading Finding Key Leaders & Building Impact Movements.

5 Steps to Launching an Impact Chapter

Pray. Ask God to open your eyes to grant you favor with people and to allow the Holy Spirit to lead you.

Decode the campus. Find out where students of

African descent gather. talk with black students about the campus, learn about existing black student groups and expose yourself to cultural events sponsored by communities of African descent.

Find an Impact person of peace. Explore possibilities with local black churches, meet with the president of the gospel choir or other existing black organizations, share the Impact vision with students already involved with Campus Crusade. You can also distribute Impact Kits at key times to meet more students.

Connect the key leader with an Impact coach. Find out who the Impact team leader is for your region by clicking here and let them know about the student(s) that God has brought across your path. They will then assign an Impact campus coach to establish an ongoing relationship with that student.

Make yourself available to resource the Impact leaders. In consultation with the Impact coach determine how you can best serve the Impact students and where there might be mutual points of connection with your Cru movement through prayer, evangelistic opportunities and social events.
