Reaching the Nations

304 words · 1 min read · Article

In brief

A short overview of the Nations Movement, Cru's ministry to Native American ('First Nations') students, describing its mission to Restore, Nurture, and Serve Native communities in light of historical trauma, high rates of suicide and addiction, and low rates of Christian faith among Native Americans. Includes contact information for launching a Nations chapter.

Reaching Native Americans Mission

Launching communities of hope on every campus where First Nations students and faculty exist. RESTORE: Fanning the flame of the gospel among every Native student so that their lives and culture can be restored by Christ .

NURTURE: Cultivating care and community to ensure that all involved will better understand and experience the gospel.

It’s a new day where many Native student s are worshipping and following Jesus Christ proud of their identity as Natives created in God’ s image.
