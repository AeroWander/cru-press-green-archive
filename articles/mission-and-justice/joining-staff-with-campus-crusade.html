To picture the structure of the Campus Ministry, it is helpful to imagine a multi-lane highway. The scope of our mission is so large we have segmented the Campus Ministry into separate, specialized, initiatives. We call them lanes. vibrant athletic programs, dynamic school spirit, or rich histories, these campuses radiate excitement and embody the academic ideal.

There is the lane that focuses on planting new ministries, Catalytic. Our focused outreach to ethnic students is the ESM (Ethnic Student Ministry) lane. Our traditional, and foundational ministries are the Staffed Campus lane. Efforts to establish campus ministries around the world are accomplished through our WSN (Worldwide Student Network) lane. And our support lanes, some call them the yellow lines between the lanes, are Human Resources, Operations and Fund Development. Staff in these lanes provide service for everything from graphics and web technology to funding and staff placement. Here you’ll find a basic overview of each of these lanes.

Salary

It is the policy of Campus Crusade for Christ International that each staff member develops a team of ministry partners to support his/her ministry. Campus Crusade’s salary structure is based on need, not on a position of leadership. All staff members, including the president, Steve Douglass, work under the same financial policies and pay scale. In order to keep our staff free from financial distractions and focused on our mission, our salary structure promotes a moderate lifestyle, longevity in ministry, and individual freedom and responsibility for staff. Each staff member starts with a base salary which is then increased based on age, the size of family, geographic location, and tenure to arrive at a maximum salary. Staff members may then request their salary to be any amount between the minimum required salary and the maximum amount. Additional salary may be requested for special needs like the purchase of a car or college tuition for children. Destino. Application Process

Here you’ll find the basic overview of the application process. While much of the work is accomplished by forms, it is anything but impersonal. Application Timeline

Step 1: Complete and submit Online Application Step 2: Complete any additional application materials as directed by the Sending & Recruiting Team.

Step 3: We will schedule a Staff Interview in your area if you haven’t already had one at a CCC conference.

Step 4: All Application materials must be received by April 1 for Summer training and November 1 for Winter training.

Step 5: You will be called for a phone interview to follow up on information received in both your initial interview and application.

Step 6: Once approved, you will receive a Pre-Acceptance decision within 6-8 weeks from the date that we received your completed application materials.

Step 7: If you receive a Pre-Acceptance to staff, a Background Check will be performed.

Step 8: Upon completion of the Background Check, you will receive your Final Acceptance decision.

Step 9: We will send you information on New Staff Training, Ministry Partner Development, and your staff account number.

Step 10: Attend New Staff Training. (Held in January/February and June/July.)
