MAIN CATEGORY: EVANGELISM

Sub-Category Heading: Teaching/Training

Sub-Category Text:

“In writing to the church in Colosse, the Apostle Paul makes this urgent request: “pray for us that God may open a door for our message. Pray that I may proclaim it clearly, as I should” (Col.4:3-4). If clarity in communicating the gospel was a concern for the apostle Paul, there’s probably good reason for us to be concerned as well. Paul, in fact, makes this urgent appeal: “Do your best to present yourself to God as one . . .who correctly handles the word of truth” (2Tim. 2:15). In this section you’ll find the training you need to communicate the gospel message clearly and effectively.
