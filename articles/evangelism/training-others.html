In brief

A short leader's guide for training others to use Soularium, a photo-based conversational evangelism tool. It explains the tool's purpose (exploring another person's story rather than forcing a gospel presentation), defines five markers of a successful conversation, and lays out a three-step training process (get ready, prep, training day) adaptable to individuals or small and large groups.

The Soularium Team

Your Role Is Critical: You Set the Stage

When you teach another person how to use Soularium, you not only shape their experience, but the experience they pass on to others and, most importantly, the experience of the person with whom they use Soularium. So it’s critical they understand how the tool was meant to be used — the philosophy and understanding of God, people, and the gospel that shaped its design.

Quite simply, how you train others matters.

Consider this: The purpose of Soularium is not “to get to the gospel”.

The Heart of Soularium

Soularium is designed to help us genuinely and sincerely explore and understand another person’s story.

We are confident that the Holy Spirit will open doors to the gospel during many Soularium conversations — perhaps even most. But it’s possible to have a successful Soularium conversation that doesn’t get to the gospel.

So this raises the question, what is a successful Soularium conversation? It’s successful if the user does these 5 things:

  • Initiates a conversation
  • Asks (open-ended) questions
  • Genuinely listens
  • Explores the other person’s life story
  • Follows the leading of the Holy Spirit

Step-by-step Training Prep: 3 Easy Steps Step 1: Get Ready

All resources are found at: http://crupressgreen.com/ soularium/

  • Download the Training Videos: “Getting Started” & “Going Deeper”
  • Download & print the article: “Training Videos Discussion Guide” (This is for you.)
  • Download & print the article: “How to Use Soularium”

(Print a copy for everyone who’s being trained.)

  • Purchase Soularium. (Ideally one per person, but could be shared by 2-3 in a training group if needed.)

Step 2: Prep

  • Watch the videos and read the articles yourself. (Don’t forget to do the action steps!)

Step 3: Training Day

  • Distribute Soularium to individuals or discussion groups.
  • Simply walk through the “Watch, Discuss, Practice, Share” steps in the article: “Training Videos Discussion Guide” (For large groups, see “Adapt to Fit Your Situation” below.)
  • Give each person a copy of the article: “How to Use Soularium” as a summary/reminder of what they’ve learned.

Adapt to Fit Your Situation

You can easily adapt the above steps to fit the number of people you’re training to use Soularium: individuals, small groups, and large groups. If it’s a larger group, have them break down in smaller groups of 3-4 to discuss the questions and apply the action steps that you walk them through from up front.

Enjoy using Soularium! ~The Creators of Soularium
