Email Series: “How to Talk About God

179 words · 1 min read · Article · For students

In brief

A short promotional flyer for an EveryStudent.com email series called "How to Talk About God," inviting students to sign up for free tips on having natural conversations about faith, using Facebook, sharing their story, and answering spiritual questions.

Evangelism toolsPersonal evangelism & gospel conversations

Everystudent.com

It’s free. Your email address will not be shared with anyone else. You can unsubscribe anytime. Would you like to learn to help others know Jesus? Just sign up: How To Talk About God. Philemon verse 6 (rsv) says...

“And I pray that the sharing of your faith, may promote the knowledge of all the good that is ours in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

A great verse, isn’t it?

How do we share our faith? How do we help someone understand “the good that is ours in Christ Jesus our Lord?”

This series will help you. It will give you ideas that have been used by many, leading thousands (literally) to begin a relationship with Jesus.

“How to Talk About God” gives you: Ways to have a comfortable, brief conversation about God.

Creative, easy ideas to use on Facebook. A personal page on EveryStudent.com, to share your story.

Help in answering those difficult, spiritual questions.

How to help someone begin a relationship with God. · Ways you could reach an entire campus or city.
