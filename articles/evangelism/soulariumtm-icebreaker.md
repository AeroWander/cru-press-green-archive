## Soularium™ Team What Is It?

Here's a constructive icebreaker to do with small groups. It will help people:

- Connect and get to know each other
- Reflect on their own faith journey
- Become comfortable talking about their story with others
- Find connections between their personal faith stories and the stories of others ASK THE ONE NON-SOULARIUM™ QUESTION:
4. As you think about your spiritual journey, what Scripture has meant the most to you? Why? INVITE PARTICIPANTS TO SHARE THEIR STORIES Give your group time to think about these questions, select images, and recall a meaningful verse.

## What Do I Do?

Here's a couple options:

Set aside 15 minutes of your group time over several weeks. Each week have one or two people tell their story using the Soularium™ images and their scripture passage. OR, have everyone tell their stories during one meeting. (As the facilitator, you may want to consider sharing your story first.)

INTRODUCE THE QUESTIONS “In a moment, you will be reflecting on four questions. Three of these questions are designed to help you tell the story of your faith journey using Soularium™.” MATERIALS REQUIRED:

Ideally, one Soularium™ deck for every two or three participants, but at minimum provide one. “Consider the following way of thinking about your story. Imagine your life right now as the final scene of a movie. If you were to choose three frames from this movie (known as your life) to summarize what has brought you to where you are spiritually, which three frames would you choose?”

3. Things are different after a turning point; which image represents a part of your life you've seen change as a result? ## The Heart of Soularium

Soularium is designed to help us genuinely and sincerely explore and understand another person’s story.

We are confident that the Holy Spirit will open doors to the gospel during many Soularium conversations — perhaps even most. But it’s possible to have a successful Soularium conversation that doesn’t get to the gospel.

So this raises the question, what is a successful Soularium conversation? It’s successful if the user does these 5 things:

- Initiates a conversation
- Asks (open-ended) questions
- Genuinely listens
- Explores the other person’s life story
- Follows the leading of the Holy Spirit Enjoy using Soularium! ~The Creators of Soularium
