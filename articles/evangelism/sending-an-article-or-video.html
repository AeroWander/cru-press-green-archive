Sending an Article or Video

293 words · 1 min read · Article · For students

In brief

A short promotional piece for EveryStudent.com and EveryStudent.info, encouraging students to email articles or videos from the site to friends as an easy, low-pressure way to share the gospel. It lists sample life questions the site's articles address and points readers to instructions for sending content.

Evangelism tools

Everystudent.info

And here’s the best part: you don’t have to send someone to a website they may never go to anyway. Every article and video at EveryStudent.com can be e-mailed with a single click to a friend or family member. “In light of our conversation yesterday, I thought you might find this article interesting or helpful.” That’s it; that’s all you’d need to say along with the attached article or video—couldn’t get an easier to share the truth, love, and hope of Jesus Christ.

It’s hard to know what life was like before Shoebox greeting cards. Where the traditional line of Hallmark card was odd, old, clunky, and corny: Shoebox was clever, personal, relevant, and tailored to the many, many nuances of life.

This is perhaps the simplest way to make sure that those you know and care about have an opportunity to know Christ. For quick instructions on sending an article or video go to: EveryStudent.info EveryStudent.com is to the gospel what Shoebox Greetings is to greeting cards. In the course of your conversations questions arise concerning: life, God, significance, purpose, suffering, relationships, sex, drugs, etc., etc. At EveryStudent.com you’ll find short, insightful treatments on most any subject germane to life. These articles address the issue, shed light on the question, and make relevant connections to God and the gospel.

Here are just a few of the questions you’ll find addressed at EveryStudent.com.

What’s my purpose in Life?

Where is God in the midst of tragedy?

Can I always know that God loves and cares about me? How can we get over wrongs done to us?

Of all the religions, why consider Christianity? Is there hope for a marriage that lasts?

Why is life so hard?

Is God Real?

Why do I drink?
