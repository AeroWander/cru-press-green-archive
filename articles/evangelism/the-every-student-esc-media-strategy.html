The ‘Every Student’ (Esc)media Strategy

By Marilyn Adamson

450 words · 2 min read · Article · For student leaders

In brief

A short article by Marilyn Adamson describing the 'Every Student' (ESC) media strategy, a campus-wide poster and ad campaign that points students to free evangelistic articles on EveryStudent.com. Explains the 'poster a week' approach, lists the 17 available campaign topics, and positions the ads as conversation starters for personal evangelism.

Evangelism toolsOutreach events & campaigns

ESC Media broadly conveys an evangelistic message to the entire campus, using posters, campus newspaper ads, and evangelistic articles.

What to Expect

Most students on campus will see your posters and ads. Campuses who use the campaigns say there is tremendous value in getting these messages out on campus, to soften the ground, to cause them to think about God. And because the campaign is visible all over campus, it’s easy to offer students the free article. You can broadly hand out articles and in personal conversations. For Christian students who have never shared their faith, it’s pretty easy to say, “Have you seen these posters on campus? Would you like a free copy of the article?” Many students are very willing to read an article, even more easily than they will engage in a conversation. And every article contains a clear presentation of the gospel, written within the context of the topic.

ESC Media Strategy

There are 17 different ESC campaigns available, on a wide variety of topics. The posters/ads can all be freely downloaded, or you can order a set of all the ads in the campaign as printed slicks.

Simply take the downloads or printed slicks to a nearby Kinko’s and photocopy them for use on campus.

“A Poster a Week” Strategy

Here’s a a simple yet powerful poster campaign strategy you can try on your campus. The strategy is to put up a different poster from the ESC Media Campaign each week providing you a consistent presence on campus throughout the year.

Each of the posters/ads offer a free evangelistic article, corresponding to that topic. You can put your own email address or phone number on the posters/ ads (before you make copies), and deliver the article to students who request one. You will also want to order a large supply of the articles to hand-out on campus and quantities for Christian students on your campus to use in personal evangelism.

Students will see a variety of compelling messages throughout the school year. Every poster points them to an article on EveryStudent.com This strategy can be a great conversation starter, making it easier for you to talk to students on campus about the Gospel message.

Even if students do not contact you, each ad promotes an evangelistic website Everystudent.com.

There are several different posters for each campaign. To get posters follow this ESC Media link.

The ESC Campaigns

The following are the topics covered by ESC Media campaigns:

African Americans & Jesus; Alcohol; Changed Life; Choosing a God; Christmas; Comparing Religions; Easter; Eating Disorders; Existence of God; Racism; Life & Death; Love & Sex; Marriage; Real Life; Peace in an Unstable World; and Tragedy & Terrorism.
