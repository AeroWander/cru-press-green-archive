Looking for an innovative way to connect with new students on campus, the Campus Ministry developed Freshman Survival Kits, or FSKs, combining the universal love of free stuff with their desire to tell others about Jesus.

And 12 years later, that simple idea continues to grow. This year alone, over 27,300 FSKs, containing evangelistic materials and novelty items, were shipped to campus ministry teams across the nation. More than 1 million Freshman Survival Kits (FSKs) containing Bibles, books, water bottles, highlighters, and a downloadable music card packaged in a laundry bag have been distributed on campuses since 1996. Campus Crusade for Christ created the Freshman Survival Kits to provide every college student with the opportunity to know Christ.

## Kits Provide Connections to Freshmen

These kits give staff members or student leaders a friendly and low-pressure opportunity to talk to students about God.
